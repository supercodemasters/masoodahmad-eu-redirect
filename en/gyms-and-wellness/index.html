Industries

IT and software for gyms, sports venues and wellness businesses

Memberships, classes and rooms in one flow.

Gyms, yoga studios, sports venues and wellness firms handle memberships, classes, instructors and rooms. When booking, payment and member register are separate systems, admin gets heavy.

Common challenges

  • Member register, payment and booking in different systems
  • Full and empty classes without overview
  • Manual handling of freezes and cancellations

What can be built

  • Booking of classes and rooms
  • Memberships with recurring payment
  • A waiting list with automatic places
  • A dashboard for occupancy and member growth

Frequently asked questions

Can members book on mobile?

Yes. Booking and cancelling happen on mobile, with reminders.

Does it work for sports clubs?

It can be adapted for clubs with training groups and venues.
