Frequently asked questions

How is sensitive data protected?

With login, roles, permissions and logging, under GDPR. Records stay in the records system.

Can you connect to our existing system?

Often, if the system has an API or can export data. That is checked first.

What does a booking flow cost?

It depends on rules and connections. After the first call you get a proposal with a price.

Does it suit a small salon or clinic?

Yes. Sometimes a ready-made service is enough; then I will say so.

Shall we have a short call?

Book a short video or phone call. The first call is free of charge.
