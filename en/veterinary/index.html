Industries

IT and software for veterinary clinics and animal care

Booking and reminders per animal, not just per owner.

Veterinary clinics, dog groomers and dog daycares have two customers at once: the animal and the owner. Vaccinations, follow-ups and treatments need reminders per animal.

Common challenges

  • Vaccination reminders done by hand
  • Phone booking takes reception time
  • Owners ask for record copies and receipts

What can be built

  • Online booking per treatment and vet
  • Vaccination and follow-up reminders per animal
  • An owner portal with receipts and documents
  • Connection to till and records where possible

Frequently asked questions

Can an owner have several animals?

Yes. Each animal gets its own history and reminders.

Does it suit dog daycare?

Yes, with places, schedules and monthly payments.
